[English](#english) · [中文](#中文)

## English

Background reading for [astrolab](https://github.com/sudoghut/astrolab), an experiment in
agent-driven astronomy research on a personal laptop. The project's operating documents
(README, ROADMAP, STATUS, AGENTS) live in the main repository; this wiki holds longer
explanatory articles.

The Atlas and every study's interactive page are online at [sudoghut.github.io/astrolab](https://sudoghut.github.io/astrolab/).

- [Five Years of Astronomy (2021–2026): A Plain-Language Review](Five-Years-of-Astronomy-2021-2026)

**Paper studies.** One article per study that starts from one or more papers: a plain-language
walkthrough of the paper, its argument checked step by step in Lean, and what astrolab can test or explore.

- [Dormant black holes in Gaia astrometric orbits](Study-001-gaia-dormant-black-holes) (Gaia BH1, BH2, BH3; study 001)
- [DESI DR2: is dark energy evolving?](Study-002-desi-bao-evolving-dark-energy) (DESI DR2 BAO, w0waCDM; study 002)
- [The Hubble tension: does host selection explain the lower JWST values?](Study-003-hubble-tension-host-selection) (SH0ES, CCHP; study 003)
- [Wide binaries: Newton or MOND at low acceleration?](Study-004-wide-binaries-newton-or-mond) (Gaia DR3 wide binaries; study 004: different sample, cannot distinguish, no power)
- [NANOGrav 15 yr: how loud is the gravitational-wave background?](Study-005-nanograv-gwb-power-law) (NANOGrav 15-yr GWB amplitude and spectral index; study 005)
- [Galaxies at z ≈ 14: too many, too bright, too early?](Study-006-z14-galaxies-number-density) (MoM-z14, JADES-GS-z14-0; study 006)
- [Black-hole masses in GWTC-4.0: a peak at 35 M☉, or just a bend?](Study-007-gwtc4-bbh-mass-35msun) (GWTC-4.0 population; study 007, concluded: R1 not reproduced as registered; 153 post hoc with a FAR fallback)
- [Has the S8 tension eased? DES Y6 against the CMB](Study-008-des-y6-s8-cmb) (DES Y6 3x2pt; study 008, reproduced: 2.67σ in S8, 2.18σ in S8–Ωm)
- [Is the Milky Way light? The Keplerian decline of its rotation curve](Study-009-mw-rotation-curve-decline) (Gaia DR3 rotation curve; study 009, reproduced under J23's prior; the mass is set by the halo shape)
- [When did the Milky Way's disc begin? Ages of a quarter of a million subgiants](Study-010-thick-disc-early-formation) (LAMOST/Gaia subgiant ages; study 010: 13 Gyr mean reproduced, split and sequence undetermined)
- [Is the Radcliffe Wave waving? Young clusters and a Galactic oscillation](Study-011-radcliffe-wave-oscillation) (Gaia DR3/APOGEE young clusters, ¹²CO; study 011, concluded: mixed)
- [The poor old heart of the Milky Way: metal-poor stars at the Galactic Centre](Study-012-poor-old-heart) (Gaia DR3 XP metallicities; study 012, concluded: not reproduced and challenged, with post-hoc qualifications)

The sources of these pages are kept in the main repository under
[`docs/wiki/`](https://github.com/sudoghut/astrolab/tree/main/docs/wiki) and reviewed there
before being published here.

## 中文

这里是 [astrolab](https://github.com/sudoghut/astrolab) 的背景阅读材料。astrolab 是一个在个人电脑上、由编程智能体（coding agent）驱动的天文学研究实验。项目的操作文档（README、ROADMAP、STATUS、AGENTS）放在主仓库；本 Wiki 存放篇幅较长的科普与说明文章。

Atlas 与每个研究的交互网页在线发布于 [sudoghut.github.io/astrolab](https://sudoghut.github.io/astrolab/)。
