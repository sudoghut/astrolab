# astrolab wiki

[English](#english) · [中文](#中文)

## English

Background reading for [astrolab](https://github.com/sudoghut/astrolab), an experiment in
agent-driven astronomy research on a personal laptop. The project's operating documents
(README, ROADMAP, STATUS, AGENTS) live in the main repository; this wiki holds longer
explanatory articles.

- [Five Years of Astronomy (2021–2026): A Plain-Language Review](Five-Years-of-Astronomy-2021-2026)

**Paper studies.** One article per study that starts from one or more papers: a plain-language
walkthrough of the paper, its argument checked step by step in Lean, and what astrolab can test or explore.

- [Dormant black holes in Gaia astrometric orbits](Study-001-gaia-dormant-black-holes) (Gaia BH1, BH2, BH3; study 001)
- [DESI DR2: is dark energy evolving?](Study-002-desi-bao-evolving-dark-energy) (DESI DR2 BAO, w0waCDM; study 002)

The sources of these pages are kept in the main repository under
[`docs/wiki/`](https://github.com/sudoghut/astrolab/tree/main/docs/wiki) and reviewed there
before being published here.
