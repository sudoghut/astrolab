M11 Radcliffe Wave: is the young-cluster wave oscillating?M11 Radcliffe 波：年轻星团组成的波在振荡吗？

A paper study of Konietzka et al. 2024 (Nature 628, 62; arXiv:2402.12596, "K24"). Using only K24's two public tables (125 young clusters, 79 clouds with ¹²CO velocities) we refit their damped-wave model and ask how firmly the data prefer a travelling over a standing wave, with tests fixed before any fit (pre-registration, README sections 1–4).对 Konietzka 等 2024（Nature 628, 62；arXiv:2402.12596，"K24"）的论文研究。只用 K24 公开的两张表（125 个年轻星团，79 个带 ¹²CO 速度的云），重新拟合他们的阻尼波模型，并用在任何拟合之前就固定的检验（预注册，README 第 1–4 节）回答：数据对行波（travelling wave）相对驻波（standing wave）的偏好有多强。

Post-hoc diagnostic (README 4.11, T-061): R-DIAG事后诊断（README 4.11，T-061）：R-DIAG

Added after the formal, R-CONV and T8-P results were seen, so everything here is post hoc. It asks where the clouds-only column-(3) difference comes from: (A) which clouds carry the likelihood difference between K24's medians and our fit; (B) refits on a sightline sample reconstructed from the Zucker et al. 2020 and Alves et al. 2020 catalogues (S, primary; S_all and S+PT reported); (C) how close our model curve is to K24's published best-fit curve; (D) refits with the origin of s free and with the Sun 20.8 pc above the midplane. The reading uses S only; the formal labels above stay as recorded.这些是在看到正式、R-CONV 与 T8-P 结果之后加的，所以这里全部是事后（post hoc）的。它问只用云的第 (3) 列差别从何而来：(A) 在 K24 中位数与我们的拟合之间，哪些云承担了似然差；(B) 用从 Zucker 等 2020 与 Alves 等 2020 星表重建的视线样本重拟合（S 为主，S_all 与 S+PT 只报告）；(C) 我们的模型曲线与 K24 公布的最佳拟合曲线有多接近；(D) 让 s 的起点自由，以及让太阳位于中面以上 20.8 pc 时重拟合。解读只用 S；上面的正式标签保持不变。
