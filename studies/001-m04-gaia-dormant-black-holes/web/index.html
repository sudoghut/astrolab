Atlas / L4 / M4

Dormant black holes in Gaia astrometric orbits

Reproducing Gaia BH1 and BH2 from DR3 non-single-star orbits alone, and a fully server-side screen of all 169,227 DR3 astrometric orbits for massive dark companions, built to rerun on Gaia DR4.

Gaia DR4 is released 2026-12-02 (archive offline from 2026-11-25). The screen is parameterised by release.

Pre-registered criteria

Reproduction: companion mass from DR3 astrometry alone

Histograms: Monte Carlo over the full DR3 covariance of (parallax, A, B, F, G, P) and the published luminous-star mass. Band: published mass ±1σ (joint astrometry + RV fits).

Screen: astrometric mass function vs period

3,343 solutions with fM > 0.25 M☉ and parallax S/N > 5 (computed on the Gaia server). Horizontal lines: fM for a dark companion of the given mass around the assumed M1.

Candidates with M2 > 3 M☉

M2 uses Gaia FLAME mass for M1 (1 M☉ if missing). Quality labels (qc) were pre-registered except where marked post-hoc. A large M2 here is a lead, not a discovery: spurious orbits and triples can mimic it.
