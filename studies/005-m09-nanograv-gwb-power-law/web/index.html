NANOGrav 15 yr: the GWB amplitude and spectral index, refitted on a laptopNANOGrav 15 年：在笔记本电脑上重拟引力波背景的振幅与谱指数

A refit of the power-law gravitational-wave background (GWB) from NANOGrav's public KDE free spectrum (factorised likelihood of Lamb, Taylor & van Haasteren 2023), compared with Agazie et al. 2023 (ApJL 951, L8) and with the literature predictions collected in L37 Table A1. Criteria are pre-registered in the study README (sections 1–4); the argument is decomposed in the wiki article.用 NANOGrav 公开的 KDE 自由谱（Lamb, Taylor & van Haasteren 2023 的分解似然）重拟幂律引力波背景（GWB），并与 Agazie 等 2023（ApJL 951, L8）以及 L37 表 A1 汇总的文献预言比较。判据预注册在研究 README 第 1–4 节；论证拆解见 Wiki 文章。

Verdicts判定

All results use L8's fiducial DMX noise model. On the free spectrum fitted with the DMGP model of dispersion-measure variations (v1.1.0, post hoc) the refit gives H = 0.31 (R1 would read "differs") and q = 0.62; see README section 6.所有结果都用 L8 基准的 DMX 噪声模型。在用色散量变化的 DMGP 模型拟合的自由谱上（v1.1.0，事后），重拟给出 H = 0.31（R1 会判"不同"）与 q = 0.62；见 README 第 6 节。
