M12 Poor old heart: Gaia XP metallicities of the oldest Milky Way starsM12 贫而老的核心：Gaia XP 金属丰度与银河系最老的恒星

A paper study of Rix et al. 2022 (ApJ 941, 45; arXiv:2209.02722, "R22"). Using only R22's two public files (Zenodo 7035810: XP metallicities of about 1.5 million giants towards the Galactic Centre, and orbits for about 1.2 million of them) we recount the metal-poor stars, refit their spatial extent with the sample's own selection, re-measure the rotation–metallicity relation and test how much these numbers lean on the metallicity scale, with tests fixed before any data were read (pre-registration, README sections 1–4).对 Rix 等 2022（ApJ 941, 45；arXiv:2209.02722，"R22"）的论文研究。只用 R22 公开的两个文件（Zenodo 7035810：银心方向约 150 万颗巨星的 XP 金属丰度，以及其中约 120 万颗的轨道），重新计数贫金属星，在考虑样本自身选择的情况下重新拟合其空间尺度，重新测量转动与金属丰度的关系，并检验这些数字在多大程度上依赖金属丰度标度；所有检验在读取任何数据之前就已固定（预注册，README 第 1–4 节）。
